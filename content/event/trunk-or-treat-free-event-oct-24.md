The party starts at 2:00 and runs until 4:00! (You can come any time after 2:00) 

The lights will be dimmed, stations with trunks will be open throughout the museum, and kiddos in their Halloween costumes can wander finding vintage Halloween decor on their scavenger hunt and be met by friendly treat-givers at the cars! When they finish the tour they will choose among different activities to do and have some refreshments.

 At 4:00 they will gather together and do a parade for the judges and costumes will be judged for some great prizes! So plan to be here at 4:00! We may even have some kiddos judge the museum adults that dressed up! 

Tell your friends! Bring a group, if you can! 

There will be non-food items available at the stations as well, for those with diet restrictions. Call 260-927-8022 if you have any questions!
