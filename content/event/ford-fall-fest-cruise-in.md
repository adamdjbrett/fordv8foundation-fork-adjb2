Wear your flannel to this end of season Cruise-in! Flannel shirts give you half price admission to the museum! Take advantage of the beautiful fall photo op we will have for you! Wear matching flannels with your Honey and sit on the straw bales in front of a beautiful vintage Ford truck, surrounded by pumpkins, mums, and orange plaid blankets!  

Enjoy refreshments from our pop-up Henry's Diner in the event center and Dirty Splash Retreat!  

With museum entrance kiddos of all ages can enjoy the vintage Halloween scavenger hunt! 

Don't miss this celebration of fall with other fun car enthusiasts!
