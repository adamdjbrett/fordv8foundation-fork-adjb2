Come out Sunday, Sept. 13, 12-3:00 to see all makes and models of cars, enjoy a hot dog and snacks, and a variety of sodas, shake-ups and other wonderful, refreshing drinks from Dirty Splash Retreat beverage trailer!  Enjoy visiting with other car enthusiasts and tour the museum! 

A special Sunday Talk will be presented by our own Ford Historian, David Sprowl, who will surprise you with interesting history on the Ford Tractors branch of the Ford Motor Company!  This presentation is included with your price of admission to the museum! A lot going on that Sunday! 

Down the Road, plan on attending our last Cruise-in for the season on Sunday, Oct. 25. It will be a fall celebration at our annual Ford Fall Fest: Fords and Flannels, although EVERY MAKE and MODEL is encouraged to attend! Wear your flannel shirt and get half price museum entrance! Inside the museum, kids of all ages can participate in the Halloween Scavenger Hunt! Enjoy fall treats, and take a family photo at our beautiful FALL themed photo op with a museum vehicle! If you have a car to show off, bring it! If you do not, come enjoy the other cars and fall festivities! We are one of the last cruise-ins of the seasons so drive that car one last time before winter! 

Please consider becoming a member of the EFV8 Foundation for only $25 a year - free museum entrance for you and a guest throughout the year, news magazine, free swap meet space are just some of the benefits! If you love cars, be a part of preserving the classic car legacy for future generations! We have many ways you can get involved! Ask us!
