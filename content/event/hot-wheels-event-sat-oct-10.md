If you are a Hot Wheels Fan, this event is for YOU! Come visit rows of Hot Wheels vendors with all price points, participate in competitive racing for prizes, non-competitive racing play area for kids, race and car-related kids activities.  Our pop-up Henry's Diner will be open selling food, snacks and drinks!  

Starts at 10:00 a.m. in the J. Windle Event Center on the Early Ford V-8 Museum Campus. 

Plan on visiting this outstanding museum while you are here! Admission will be the regular price, at $10 Adults, $8 Seniors 55+   $8 Veterans/Military    $6 Students   Under 7 FREE           There will be a museum scavenger hunt for kids of all ages when you tour the museum!
